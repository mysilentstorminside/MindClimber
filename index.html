How to play

Your goal is to reach the summit (step 30) before everyone else.

  • On your turn, pick a category, then a difficulty:
    🟢 Easy (+1/−1) · 🔵 Medium (+2/−2) · 🟠 Hard (+3/−3)
  • Right answer → you climb up. Wrong answer → you slide down.
  • Everyone answers the same question. You also have a player clock — if it runs out, you're out.
  • Up to 5 players online, or play solo.

The golden trophy 🏆 waits at the summit. The first player to reach step 30 wins.
